Biscuit Manufacturing ERP Prototype

Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.

Included:
- Dashboard
- Recipe Management with version/approval concept
- Production order with quantity scaling
- Inventory
- Manufacturing vs management full costing
- Accounts / General Ledger examples
- Input/output sales tax
- Reports

This is a front-end prototype with demo data. It is not connected to a live database, FBR, payroll, bank, or accounting system. Tax rates are illustrative and must be configured for the actual product and applicable law.
