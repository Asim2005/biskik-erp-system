5-Factor Variance Engine

The standard is frozen onto the order when it is planned. On completion the difference is split five ways, so "it cost more" becomes a specific, answerable question. On the demo run the five add to Rs. 33,628.99 - exactly what work in process held above standard.

• Usage: (actualQty − stdQty) × stdRate
• Price: (actualRate − stdRate) × actualQty
• Yield: (planned − good) × stdUnitCost
• Labour and overhead: actual absorbed − standard
Moving Average Inventory

Every receipt re-averages the rate; every issue leaves at the current average. Because issues leave at average and finished goods are capitalised at standard, the gap between the two is not lost - it is what the variance accounts report.

New Rate = (Old Value + Inbound Value) / (Old Qty + Inbound Qty)
Segregation of duties

Fifteen roles across R&D, procurement, warehouse, production, sales and finance. What a person may do is decided by the action, not the department, and the API checks it on every request - hiding a button is never the only control.

• The author of a recipe cannot approve it
• The buyer who raises an order cannot approve it
• Approval is capped by value, per role
